What is being asked?

This question asks the learner to listen to a short narrative and then retell the narrative using their own words.

What is being tested?

This question tests listening and speaking. It assesses understanding of a short narrative, accuracy of complex grammatical structures, and oral fluency.

B2 Grammar Targets:

  • • Past Perfect & Continuous narrations
  • • Future Perfect (will have completed)
  • • Time clauses with by, before, once, as soon as
