What is being asked?

This question asks the learner to listen to a short narrative and then retell the narrative using their own words in English.

What is being tested?

This question tests listening comprehension, task completion, grammar accuracy (B2 narrative structures), and English spoken fluency.

B2 Evaluation Criteria:

  • • Task & Content (0-4 pts): Mentions characters, main problem, actions, and ending.
  • • Grammar Accuracy (0-3 pts): Past Perfect, Continuos, or Future Perfect structures.
  • • Fluency & Cohesion (0-3 pts): Connectors (although, because, by), vocabulary range, and spoken length.
